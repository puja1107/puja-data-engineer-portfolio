01 / ABOUT

More than pipelines.

I work across the full data engineering lifecycle: ingesting data, orchestrating processing, transforming and modelling datasets, validating data quality, supporting production workloads, and delivering data to analytics platforms.

My strongest experience is on Google Cloud, particularly BigQuery, Dataflow and Cloud Composer. I also work with Python, SQL, Apache Beam, Terraform and GitLab CI/CD, alongside Qlik Sense and ODAG for analytics use cases.

A significant part of my recent work has been in telecom data platforms, including CDR, subscriber, device, site and network usage data. I enjoy solving the less visible engineering problems too: reliability, monitoring, operational recovery, cost, permissions and automation.
